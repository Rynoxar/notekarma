Terms of Service

NoteKarma is a student project. Last updated: 14 October 2026.

Using the app

You sign in with a Google account. One person should use one account.

What you may upload

  • Only notes you made yourself, or that you have the right to share.
  • No copyrighted books, no offensive, illegal or misleading content.

Karma points

Karma points are virtual points inside the app. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money.

Admin rights

The admin may remove posts and block accounts that break these rules or misuse the app.
