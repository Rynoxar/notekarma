Privacy Policy

NoteKarma is a student project for sharing class notes. Last updated: 14 October 2026.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address.
  • The notes (PDF files), titles and descriptions you upload.
  • Your likes, downloads and karma points.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the app: to sign you in, show your name next to notes you share, count karma, and let the admin remove spam or block misuse.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and file storage). We do not sell your data and do not share it with advertisers.

Who can see what

Your name and the notes you upload are visible to other signed-in users. Your email address is visible only to you and the admin.

Deleting your data

You can delete your own posts in the app. To remove your account and data, contact the developer through the project page: github.com/Rynoxar/notekarma.
