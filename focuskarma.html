How a session works

Stake

Pick a 25 or 50 minute room. Your 5 Karma goes into the room’s pool when you join.

Focus

Stay on the room tab. Everyone in the room is doing the same work alongside you.

Settle

The timer ends. Finishers get their stake back plus a share of the Karma left by anyone who quit.

What you win, what you lose

You finish

  • Your 5 Karma comes back
  • You earn 1 Karma for finishing, plus a share of any forfeited stakes
  • The room sees you completed it

You leave early

  • Your 5 Karma is cut
  • It is split between students who finished
  • Switching tabs or leaving the room counts as leaving

Built for people who struggle to start

Procrastination, ADHD and studying alone are hard to beat with willpower. A small, real cost for quitting and other people working in the same room make starting easier and staying easier.
